The shadow scale draws in the nearest gray role's colour, or `--line-neutral-*` without `line-themes`, at the Open Props per-layer opacity. Dark mode follows `color-scheme` through `light-dark()`, and the new `--line-shadow-strength-dark` (25%) sets its strength. `--line-shadow-color` is optional and takes an opaque colour. The shadow tokens are declared in every theming scope, so a rule that overrides one everywhere targets `html, [data-accent], [data-gray]`.
