Add `<line-button-group>` at `@websublime/line-components/button-group`, a toolbar named by its host `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. It gives its slotted `<line-button>` and `<line-icon-button>` children one Tab stop with arrow-key focus (`orientation` `horizontal` or `vertical`, mirrored right-to-left, Home and End, wrap with `loop`). It skips disabled and hidden buttons, moves focus on when the focused button is disabled, hidden or removed, and squares the corners between its buttons (`--line-button-group-radius`, `--line-button-group-gap`). The `line` A2UI catalog, `catalog.json` and the frontend tools of `@websublime/line-genui/catalog` gain `ButtonGroup`, which `<line-a2ui-surface>` renders only when the node carries `accessibility.label`. `<line-button>` and `<line-icon-button>` now stretch their inner button (`::part(root)`) to fill the host, so every button of a vertical group is as wide as the widest. Any layout that stretches a button stretches it too: in a default vertical `<line-stack>` or an A2UI `Column`, Buttons and IconButtons now render full width; set `align` on the Stack (`start`, `center` or `end`) to keep their content width.
