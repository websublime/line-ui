## Method (Phase 1 spec locators)
- A locator's intended text is the line it named when it was written. For each locator, the script walks the
  component spec's history back to the earliest commit whose matching line still holds the same locator token, reads
  the Phase 1 spec at that commit, and maps that line number to the spec at AM-116 through a line diff of the two
  versions. A line edited in place since then maps through its unchanged neighbours (61 locators, each checked by
  hand: same item, list grown in place).
- Each mapped locator was then checked against its label (an SD, AM or ledger-row id, a § number, the Status line).
  The labels that did not match name a neighbouring item, such as the "notes" range after a C-row label or the reason
  rows SD38 lists; in each, the text at the new line equals the text the locator named when written.
- Two shortcuts failed and were dropped: `git blame` dates a line by its last edit, which for lines touched by a PRD
  realignment is later than the Phase 1 spec locator on the same line.
- Locators the sentence ties to no document by name were reviewed one by one: four `§6.G.3, \`:1405\`` locators are
  Phase 1 spec locators and moved; the rest cite test files, `bun.lock`, research or upstream sources and stay.
- `0021-aspect-ratio.md` keeps `:257` and `:258` in its Spec drift record of a dispatch locator, which is record.
- The component specs' ledger locators (`docs/plans/01-tasks-core-primitives.md:N`) are outside O6 and unchanged.
