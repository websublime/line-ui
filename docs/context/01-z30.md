## Investigation (2026-11-27, Z30Investigate; repo `contrastRatio`, Radix 3.0.0, sRGB and P3)
- Text on a step-9 solid in shipped code: Button and Chip `primary` labels (16px, weight 500: normal-size text);
  IconButton `primary` icons (non-text, 3:1). Badge, Alert, Avatar, Kbd paint no text on a solid. Semantic
  `--line-{role}-contrast` tokens alias hue tokens, so a 31-hue × 2-mode gate covers every role.
- Today: 28/62 pairs pass 4.5:1 (worst orange 2.97, both modes); 34 fail: 14 saturated hues in both modes and the 6
  grays in light. The better of black and white per hue × mode passes 62/62 (worst plum/white 4.75; P3 62/62, no
  flips). A static per-hue value passes only 56/62: the 6 grays need black in light and white in dark.
- Other options measured: own-scale text (best 35/62), white on a darker Radix step (62/62 sRGB, 2 P3 flips, large
  colour shifts), white on an OKLCH-darkened solid (62/62 sRGB, P3 unmeasured, non-Radix colours), large text at 3:1
  (contradicts D29).

## Decision (Miguel, 2026-11-27)
- Option (1) over (2) a darker Radix step and (3) an OKLCH-derived solid: `PER_HUE_CONTRAST` takes the better of
  black and white per hue on step 9 (20 black hues), and the 6 grays become mode-aware `light-dark(#000, #fff)`. Step
  9 stays Radix; no component code changes; `scripts/validate-contrast.mjs` moves to 4.5:1 with the orange allowlist
  removed. Labels turn black on the 14 saturated hues (incl. success, danger, info) and on the light-mode grays,
  departing from Radix Themes' white-on-saturated table, which PRD §9.2 adopted verbatim. Phase 00 spec AM-092
  (supersedes AM-014's threshold and allowlist) and PRD v0.8.39 (§5.2.1, §9.2, §9.6) before the code.
