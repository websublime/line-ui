## Facts
- The ring is `2px solid var(--line-accent-8)` with a 2px offset; it sits on the page or component surface (gray
  steps 1–2 and others), not on the accent fill. R10 measured it below 3:1 on 196 of 372 accent × gray × mode
  combinations (min 1.86:1).
- Nothing gates the ring's contrast today.

## Open questions (for the decide step, after the investigation)
- Which step or composition (a different accent step, a mode-dependent step, a two-tone ring, a gray fallback) meets
  3:1 against every surface it can sit on, in all combinations, and what it costs visually and in the token API.

## Investigation (2026-11-27, Z31Investigate; repo `contrastRatio`, Radix 3.0.0, sRGB and P3)
- 372 = 31 accents × 6 grays × 2 modes (`GRAY_HUES` has 6 grays). Surfaces a ring sits on: gray-1, gray-2, gray-3,
  accent-2, accent-3 and the semantic step-3 fills (Alert), with nested `data-accent` scopes. Components apply no

  too; only a two-tone ring passes where the ring lies over a neighbouring primary button in a ButtonGroup.
